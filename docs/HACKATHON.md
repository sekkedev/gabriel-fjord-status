# Hackathon Store Lungegårdsvann

- **When:** Saturday 31 October 2026, 10:00 to 15:00
- **Where:** UiB, Faculty of Fine Art, Music and Design (KMD), indoor balcony overlooking Store Lungegårdsvann
- **Event page:** https://www4.uib.no/om-uib/arrangementer/hackathon-store-lungegardsvann
- **Sign-up (Luma):** https://luma.com/q12vu217
- **Organizer:** UiB, Geophysical Institute
- **Language:** Norwegian and English

## Goal

Make Store Lungegårdsvann so clean you can swim in it, and full of life below the surface. Focus suggested by the organizers: data from the Gabriel buoy. Their own example ideas:

- Visualizations of the Gabriel data
- "Gabby", an automated Instagram account posting the fjord's condition every day

## Practical

- Free and open to everyone, no technical background needed. Kids 8 to 18 can come with a parent.
- Bring a laptop (or phone), your own lunch. Coffee and water provided.
- Prizes: 10,000 NOK pool for original, useful or fun entries. The winning idea may be shown on Bybanen.
- Lovable gives every participant 100 free credits.

## Background: Fjord to the People

The hackathon is part of a wider effort by UiB, Bergen kommune and partners to restore Store Lungegårdsvann and to make the public feel connected to it. Rain runoff from the city is the main remaining problem: it can carry bacteria and pollution into the fjord.

## Fair play

The rules don't mention prep, but entries are meant to be built on the day. This prototype was made beforehand as a concept sketch. Plan: say so openly when presenting, and build or extend on the day rather than only showing up with a finished product.

## Result (31 Oct 2026)

Far fewer people took part than the organisers expected. The judges liked the concept and asked a lot of questions about it. The 10,000 NOK prize pool was split rather than going to one entry, and this entry got a share. The organisers want to link sekke.dev from their website.

Built on the day, on top of the sketch: the descent and ascent chart (the slow descent is the more accurate reading), picking a dive in the heatmap, the silent Bybanen screen at `/screen/`, and Gabby's mascot face.
